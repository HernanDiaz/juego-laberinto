DESCRIPCIÓN (4000)
Twists of Life is a pixel-art maze game where the path changes every turn, just like life.

Push the loose tile in from the edge of the board to shift rows and columns, open a way to your goals and block your rivals.

• Three stages of life: youth, adulthood and golden years. Reach your goals (a diploma, a motorbike, a wedding, a house, the trip of your dreams...) and get back home to retire before anyone else.
• Money and events: collect coins, pay for the goals that cost money and take your chances on the "?" squares: inheritances, lotteries, fines... or an unexpected move!
• Rivals with personality: the ambitious one, the adventurer and the laid-back one each play their own way.
• 8 levels with stars, from the neighbourhood to golden retirement, each with its own board and rules.
• Free play against 1, 2 or 3 rivals.
• Multiplayer: up to 4 people on the same phone, or each on their own phone over Wi-Fi.
• Pixel art, chiptune music and 11 languages.

Completely free: every level included, no ads, no in-app purchases and no data collection.
